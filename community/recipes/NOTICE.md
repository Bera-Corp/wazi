# Original community recipe contributions

Original recipe/configuration files explicitly submitted under the community
contribution agreement use Apache-2.0, whose text is in LICENSE. This designation
does not license the Wazi application or relicense upstream software, copied
configuration, images or third-party setup scripts.

Portable archives must carry licenses.json with the original-recipe license,
applicable upstream license texts and preserved notices. Signing a recipe does
not replace license review. No portable community release is published by this
directory's existence.
