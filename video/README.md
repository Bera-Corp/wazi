# Wazi launch film workspace

Working title: **Small gladiator. Serious power.**

Current brief: a three-minute film beginning with a cute, short, big-eyed
female AI gladiator dragging an oversized weapon into an arena. A visible
Meta AI power source activates; she controls the weapon with her mind. The
other gladiators turn and freeze in surprise. A match cut reveals Wazi.

This folder is separate from product implementation so inspiration, script
iterations and production work can grow together.

## Start here

- [Transcript v01](transcript-v01.md): the master draft, with timecodes,
  narration, character dialogue, motion direction, screen text and sound.
- [Voiceover v01](voiceover-v01.md): the same spoken lines without production
  directions, for a timed read-through.
- [Timing check](timing-v01.md): scene durations and spoken-word counts;
  a recording is still needed to confirm delivery.
- [Production notes](production-notes.md): character, visual language,
  product coverage, evidence requirements and revision decisions.
- [Inspiration inbox](inspiration/README.md): add references as we develop
  the direction. Nothing needs to be generated or locked yet.
- [Storyboards](storyboards/README.md): later shot sketches and animatics.
- [Assets](assets/README.md): later approved artwork, motion and audio assets.

## How we iterate

1. Refine the story and spoken transcript first.
2. Add inspiration with a short note about what should influence the film.
3. Lock character design and a small set of visual rules.
4. Make a low-cost timed animatic before final animation.
5. Capture the actual product journey and replace concept-only screens as
   implementation evidence becomes available.
6. Record narration, animate, edit and rehearse the complete three minutes.

Keep versions (`v01`, `v02`, ...) so accepted lines and ideas are recoverable.
The master transcript governs the voiceover copy. Exact edit duration is
180 seconds; spoken pacing must be confirmed with a recorded read-through.

The [four judging pillars](../docs/JUDGING_PILLARS.md) guide every revision:
Impact 30%, Meta AI Tooling 25%, Technical Depth & Execution 20%, Demo 25%.
The owner's three-minute brief supersedes earlier 110–130 second planning
targets for this film; final competition submission limits still need checking.
