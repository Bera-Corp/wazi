# Wazi film production notes — v01

## Creative direction

The opening communicates a small builder wielding unusually powerful tools.
Her intention drives the action; Meta AI is visibly the source of extra
power. Wazi is introduced as the product that makes that power useful in a
controlled software lifecycle. The arena is a metaphor, not a claim about
physical AI or a working world-model integration.

Provisional visual style: stylised 3D with tactile sand and metal, soft
shapes, exaggerated scale and expressive animation. This is an editable
assumption until inspiration arrives, not a locked art direction.

Character: petite adult female AI gladiator, big expressive eyes, practical
slightly oversized armour, determined and quietly funny. The large weapon
is a mechanical warhammer with a recognisable blue power core. She drags it
with effort, then controls it through concentration. No attack is needed:
the lift and reaction sell the transformation.

Keep the Meta AI name readable at activation. Start with a text-labelled
core rather than an invented official logo. Brand assets, music, fonts and
external artwork need recorded source/licensing before final publication.
Do not imply Meta sponsorship or product certification.

## Continuity rules

- Same heroine, armour, weapon silhouette and blue core throughout.
- Warm arena colours; the same blue accent carries into Wazi's product UI.
- Motion follows intention -> power -> controlled result.
- Use a match cut from the core to a product ring, avoiding a hard switch
  into a slide deck. The emotional story continues into the user journey.
- Other gladiators show surprise and curiosity, without competitor logos.
- One application identity persists from import to deployment and outcome.
- Use readable type, captions and high contrast; sound never carries a
  critical status alone. Keep decorative HUD graphics out of the app flow.

## Judging pillars built into the film

| Pillar | Weight | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 30% | Small-team pain, one understandable task and the useful application outcome; real before/after evidence when available |
| Meta AI Tooling | 25% | Opening power source plus a central repair workflow showing a material model contribution; a logo alone is insufficient |
| Technical Depth & Execution | 20% | Visible gates and actual contract/CI evidence; describe runtime work honestly |
| Demo | 25% | Memorable opening, continuous product journey, readable footage and polished pacing; animation cannot substitute for the working product |

Weight is not a literal seconds allocation. Strong product footage can
support several pillars at once. The animated opening is limited to 22
seconds so the product, impact and evidence retain most of the film.

Upgrade the script to a stronger evidence-led final cut as features ship:
replace future-tense lines with demonstrated actions, swap concept screens
for genuine captures and spend the saved exposition time on the working
app and measured result. Never change tense merely to make the pitch sound
more complete. Time-compress or replay long operations with visible labels.

The script's “Today” status is a writing baseline, not an evergreen claim.
Reconcile it against the submitted commit and actual captures at picture lock.
The final official problem statement and video-length rule are still source
checks; the owner has requested a 180-second creative master.
